The second instrument, verified against the public vehicle register. Measured 2026-09-12.

Why a vehicle and not a lease: the rule is that every external call hits a real service, and
four measured limits closed every other door. The ledger's token is scoped to invoices — asking
it for anything else answers invalid_scope. The secret ring is at a documented ceiling of five
with no room for a sixth. The enclave spends four of five http calls. And verify.ts is pinned by
its own tests to exactly three requests.

These two services need no credential at all, which is what makes them fit.

--- the finding that justifies the plan: the threshold does not transfer ----
  two identical trucks, one vin apart
    agreement                 6 of 7
    threshold for a vehicle   7
    threshold for an invoice  6   (measured: 903 pairs, one false match)
    collision at 7            false   correct: two vehicles, two liens
    collision at 6            true    the false refusal an inherited threshold would have produced

  the same seven texts under two instrument types

    lienId as equipment       0xbeb8d2d051baec49b0ad64d76b146eda97afada93664f9a6ab2081a897509dc3
    lienId as invoice         0xc2901eaf644da70c4dd60f4e64b424270d63d7b8cd196581342fc13a84ab672a
    claimId as equipment      0x0090bbf711ce11f22a53fbeca1bde24872cd6e946af38e7ec1c005cede898a40
    claimId as invoice        0xa1e7d1501629c8c3ab710d39f9f9e1069583b405e4f15542c66a09fd318ae5fe

Six of seven is measured — brute-forced against 903 pairs of real invoices from one ledger, one
false match. It does not transfer. Two unrelated invoices from one book agree on three, the three
the ledger fixes; two vehicles financed by the same creditor to the same obligor on the same terms
agree on SIX, differing only in the number. Inheriting six would have refused the second truck of a
fleet as a double pledge of the first, and the borrower would have experienced an unexplained no.

Seven is safe here for a structural reason rather than a measured one, and the difference is stated
rather than blurred: a vin is seventeen fixed characters with a check digit, and canonicalisation —
upper case, strip anything that is not a letter or a number — loses nothing. The legitimate
reformatting that forced six down for invoices does not exist for this type. Six renderings of one
vehicle were checked and every one still agrees on seven.

There is no measured false-match rate for vehicles, and this does not borrow the invoices' number.

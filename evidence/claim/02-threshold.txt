How the threshold was chosen, 2026-09-10.

No published work supplies a value. The nearest source — reliable homomorphic matching for
fuzzy labeled PSI — fixes k = 2 and states outright that k is a cost parameter rather than an
accuracy one: raising it inflates per-pair work and the soundness bound, while the accept
probability is lowered by token rounds instead. Its sixty-four components are hash subsamples
of a face embedding evaluated on face datasets. Two of seven semantic invoice fields would be
currency plus jurisdiction, which every claim from one ledger shares, so that value is not
transferable and is not transferred.

What is transferable is the method, from the earlier paper by the same authors: fix the
threshold by brute force against a real corpus, with a target taken from what the domain
requires rather than from theory.

--- the measurement, over the seeded corpus ----------------------------------

  worst legitimate reformatting        6 of 7    invoice number without its prefix
  every other reformatting             7 of 7    absorbed by canonicalisation
  second real invoice, same ledger     3 of 7    currency, issuer and country only

Three of the seven are constants of the ledger, so three is the floor for any two claims from
one book, not zero. That leaves the threshold free to be 4, 5 or 6.

The target: the largest threshold that still accepts every reformatting real data produces.
Larger is safer — near-collision squatting requires the attacker to guess more of someone
else's claim — and no observed variant falls below six, so a lower threshold buys nothing.

  k = 6

Seven is refused: it would reject the prefix-less invoice number, a legitimate reformatting.

What this measurement cannot support: a false-match RATE. One negative sample is one sample.
The rate, and how few samples it rests on, belong to the measurement plan.

--- measured by this code, against the seeded corpus ---

7/7  collision as required  invoice number with a separator
6/7  collision as required  invoice number without its prefix
7/7  collision as required  tax id without separators
7/7  collision as required  amount written with a decimal separator
7/7  collision as required  due date in day-first format
7/7  collision as required  lowercase country
3/7  clear     as required  a second real invoice: different debtor, number, amount bucket and due date

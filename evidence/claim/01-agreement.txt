Component agreement against the canonical claim, seven components.

Three of the seven — currency, issuerTaxId, country — are properties of the
ledger, so any two claims from this book agree on them before anything about
the debt is compared. Three is the floor, not zero.

7/7  must match      invoice number with a separator
      differs on: none
6/7  must match      invoice number without its prefix
      differs on: invoiceNumber
7/7  must match      tax id without separators
      differs on: none
7/7  must match      amount written with a decimal separator
      differs on: none
7/7  must match      due date in day-first format
      differs on: none
7/7  must match      lowercase country
      differs on: none
3/7  MUST NOT match  a second real invoice: different debtor, number, amount bucket and due date
      differs on: debtorTaxId, invoiceNumber, amountBucket, dueDate

The gap between 6 and 3 is the room a threshold has to live in.

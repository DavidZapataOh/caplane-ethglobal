Why fixed widths: the naive encoding collided on this project's own data. Captured from
the run before the fix, using the tax number the ledger stores and the invoice number it
assigned:

  A (the seeded invoice):       11000111000ORC1043242026-12-31AUDE1218A28743747ECBFB5252092825083AU
  B (one character shifted):    11000111000ORC1043242026-12-31AUDE1218A28743747ECBFB5252092825083AU
  identical: true
  
  Both claims are valid: the tax number is the one the ledger stores and the invoice
  number is the one it assigned. Joining the components as strings gives one lien id
  for two different claims, and the second borrower is refused as already encumbered.

The canonical components are not repeated here — they are already in the corpus. What is
published is the lien id, which is a hash of the whole tuple and does not invert.

without an account" while the registry — the one contract that sentence tells a reader to open —
was not verified. The redeploy earlier the same day replaced it and nothing re-verified.

  GUID     e7170ee0ce4cab4593970ef5c4ebf7d0d62ae19b6aa56ec1

The four arguments are read from contracts/abi/deployments.arc-testnet.json, not retyped. They are
constructor arguments to immutables with no setter, so a wrong one here would not fail to verify —
it would verify a contract that is not the one deployed.

Full match, not partial: the metadata hash agrees, so the bytecode on chain came from this source
at these settings. That is what makes the explorer path reproducible rather than decorative — a
stranger recompiles and compares, and reads the four immutables that pin who may write, by
themselves.

--- one measurement that was wrong, recorded so nobody repeats it ------------

`/api/v2/smart-contracts/<addr>/methods-read` answers 404 on this Blockscout — v11.3.0 — for all
four contracts, verified or not. It is the wrong route, not a broken read tab. What the tab is
built from is the `abi` field above.

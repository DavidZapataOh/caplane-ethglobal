Signing on Arc, executed 2026-09-10. The static evidence said this would work; it half did,
and the half that failed is the finding.

--- broadcasting is gated per app and per chain --------------------------------

$ wallets().ethereum().sendTransaction(walletId, { caip2: 'eip155:5042002', ... })
  401 {"error":"App is not authorized to transact on chain eip155:5042002"}

The type system permits any chain — `Caip2` is a bare `string` with no whitelist — and that is
what the desk research saw. It is not the whole gate. Authorisation to broadcast is a
per-application setting, and the type gives no hint of it. This is the difference between
evidence that is strong and evidence that is executed.

--- signing is not gated, and that is what the architecture needs --------------

$ wallets().ethereum().signTransaction(walletId, { params: { transaction: { chain_id: 5042002, ... } } })
  -> signed_transaction, broadcast by us over the chain's own RPC

  hash        0x62582fc59e7c533e39fa20795810b9d5e1a9dd73bacd08065c1d94efa7bbd03e
  explorer    https://testnet.arcscan.app/tx/0x62582fc5...
  status      0x1
  from        0xb404f14dbf46dec0ec6cb86b0407b3704a835cb8   (the Privy wallet)
  blockNumber 61438153
  gasUsed     21000
  effectiveGasPrice  21 gwei

`signTransaction` takes no `caip2` — it is chain-agnostic by construction, and the chain id
travels inside the transaction. So the signer works on 5042002 and we broadcast ourselves.

That was written into the plan as a fallback to exercise "in case it is ever needed". It was
needed on the first run. Exercising the fallback before depending on it is the whole reason
there was one to fall back to.

--- the base-fee floor is real -------------------------------------------------

Effective gas price came back at 21 gwei against a 20 gwei floor. Fee fields are `snake_case`
(`max_fee_per_gas`, `gas_limit`); camelCase does not compile, and had it slipped through as an
untyped object the transaction would have gone out underpriced and been dropped with no error
and no receipt — which reads exactly like "Privy cannot sign on Arc".

--- funding ------------------------------------------------------------------

A fresh wallet holds nothing and gas on Arc is paid in the native asset, so the wallet was
funded from the deployer before either path could run. Returned afterwards.

=== Fee-floor behaviour, settled empirically 2026-09-10 ===

Circle's own docs contradict each other:
  gas-and-fees.md     : lists 'transaction underpriced' as a returned RPC error
  evm-differences.md  : 'silently dropped by the mempool. They produce no error receipt
                         and never appear in a block.'

Measured with a funded key, 1 gwei against a 20 gwei floor:
  eth_sendRawTransaction  -> ACCEPTED, returned 0x2f040d99...6368ba29
  receipt after 122 blocks-> null
  getTransactionByHash    -> null (gone from the mempool entirely)

VERDICT: evm-differences.md is right. The RPC accepts it, nothing is mined,
and the transaction vanishes. A deploy script that does not pin maxFeePerGas
above 20 gwei fails with no diagnostic whatsoever.

Nonce is not consumed: 1 (unchanged).
